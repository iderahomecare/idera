WELCOME TO IDERA

Care that feels like family.

IDERA Residential Care Home was born from love, personal experience and a desire to make a difference in the lives of older people.

Inspired by the experience of caring for a late mother and understanding the worries families face when seeking dependable care for ageing loved ones in Nigeria, IDERA was created to offer more than accommodation: a place built on trust, compassion, professionalism and dignity.

Our ambition is to create a home where older people are valued, respected and made to feel at home, while families have greater peace of mind even when distance keeps them apart.

“We understand the worry because we have lived it. We created IDERA to become the place we wished we had found for our own loved ones.”

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

How can I find out more about IDERA?

Contact our team by phone, WhatsApp or email. We will be happy to answer your questions and explain our care approach.

Can family members arrange a visit?

Yes. Please contact us to discuss a suitable time. A visit is arranged only after our team confirms the details.

Can families living outside Nigeria stay in touch?

We understand how important communication is for families living abroad. Please speak with our team about available family update arrangements.

How do I make an appointment?

Use the appointment link below or contact us directly. Your request is not confirmed until a member of our team responds.
