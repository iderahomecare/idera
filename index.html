Welcome to IDERA

Care That Feels Like Home

IDERA Residential Care Home was inspired by a personal experience of caring for a loved one and the understanding that families, especially those living abroad, need a trustworthy and compassionate care home for their loved ones in Nigeria.

We are committed to creating a welcoming environment where residents are treated with kindness, dignity and respect. Our approach is centred on individual needs, meaningful relationships and the reassurance that every resident matters.

At IDERA, care is more than a service. It is a commitment to making people feel valued, safe, respected and at home.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to some questions families may have about IDERA.

What is IDERA Residential Care Home?

IDERA is a residential care home focused on providing a welcoming environment, compassionate support and dignity for residents.

Where is IDERA located?

We are located at 7 Kennedy Thompsone Street, New Road, Awoyaya, Lekki Expressway, Lagos, Nigeria.

How can I enquire about admission?

You can contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email, or submit an enquiry through the appointment form on this website.

Can families living abroad contact IDERA?

Yes. Families living abroad can contact our team by phone, WhatsApp or email to discuss their loved one's care needs.

Can I visit before making a decision?

Please contact our team to discuss arranging a visit and learning more about the home.
