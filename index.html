The heart behind IDERA

Our Story — A Personal Experience. A Greater Purpose. A Commitment to Care.

Idera Residential Care Home was born from a place of love, personal experience and a deep desire to make a difference in the lives of older people.

The inspiration behind Idera came from a personal journey of caring for a late mother and witnessing the challenges many families face when trying to find reliable care for their ageing parents in Nigeria.

Living abroad while having an elderly parent back home can be emotionally challenging. Like many families in the diaspora, we wanted the reassurance that our mother was safe, comfortable and receiving the care she deserved. However, finding dependable care was not always easy. We often had to make daily calls to check on her welfare, constantly worrying whether her needs were being properly met.

Through conversations with friends and other families living abroad, it became clear that this was not just our experience. Many families shared the same concerns, anxieties and desire to find a place where their parents could be genuinely cared for.

There was a need for more than just elderly accommodation: a home built on trust, compassion, professionalism and dignity.

That realisation became the foundation of Idera Residential Care Home. Our ambition is to create a place where older people are not simply looked after but are valued, respected, celebrated and made to feel at home. A place where families can have confidence that their loved ones are receiving compassionate and professional attention, even when distance keeps them apart.

Guided by a Level 5 Diploma in Health and Social Care and inspired by UK care practices, we are committed to bringing a higher standard of elderly care to Nigeria while preserving the warmth, respect and family values that define our culture.

Idera is more than a care home; it is a promise born from personal experience. A promise that no family should have to live with constant worry about the care of their ageing loved ones, and that every older person deserves to age with dignity, comfort, love and respect.

“We understand the worry because we have lived it. We created IDERA to become the place we wished we had found for our own loved ones.”
— Why We Started
